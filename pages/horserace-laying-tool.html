Lay Scoring Method

Trainer Score (0–40 pts)

Based on the trainer's win strike rate. Lower win rates score higher. A trainer with <5% wins scores 40; a 20%+ trainer scores 0. Trainers with fewer than 20 recent runners get a +5 unknown penalty.

Form Score (0–40 pts)

Analyses the horse's last 6 runs. Finishes 6th+ score 5 pts each; falls, pulled up, unseated (0/F/P/U/R) score 8 pts each. Wins and places (1–3) score nothing. Fewer than 3 runs adds a +10 unknown penalty.

Odds Adjustment (0–20 pts)

Shorter-priced horses with bad fundamentals represent bigger market errors. A horse at evens (2.0) with poor form/trainer scores 20; a horse at 8/1 (9.0) scores 0. The edge is in the discrepancy.
